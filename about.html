OUR JOURNEY & HERITAGE

4 Decades of Precision Engineering Excellence

Founded in 1984 in Patiala, Punjab, Vastech Cutting Tools has grown from a specialized local tool shop into a globally acknowledged manufacturer of high-precision broaches and custom broaching machinery.

We leverage state-of-the-art CNC grinding technology, high-grade imported speed steels (M2, M35, M42, ASP Powder Metallurgy), and rigorous quality inspection protocols to ensure every tool we produce delivers unmatched tool life and micron-level profile accuracy.

Global Standards

Our Mission

To empower automotive, aerospace, defense, and industrial manufacturers worldwide by delivering cutting-edge broaching tools that minimize downtime, optimize machining cycle time, and guarantee uncompromising quality standard.

Our Vision

To be the global benchmark in broach design and tooling innovation, leading through continuous investment in smart manufacturing technology, sustainable processes, and customer-first technical support.

STATE-OF-THE-ART PLANT

Our Manufacturing Infrastructure

Equipped with high-end machinery and metrology instrumentation to meet tight tolerances.

Multi-Axis CNC Profile Grinders

Our shop floor features modern multi-axis CNC broach profile grinding machines equipped with automated wheel dressers, achieving tooth profile tolerances within ±0.002 mm.

  • Helical & Straight Broach Grinding
  • In-process laser measurement systems
  • Sub-micron surface finish delivery

Computerized Vacuum Heat Treatment

Controlled-atmosphere salt bath and vacuum tempering furnaces ensure optimal hardness (63 to 68 HRC) without surface decarburization or thermal distortion.

  • Cryogenic treatment for micro-grain refinement
  • Consistent core toughness & wear resistance

Precision Quality Inspection Lab

Temperature-controlled metrology laboratory equipped with high-resolution profile projectors, lead & pitch measuring equipment, and hardness testers.

  • 100% Tooth-by-tooth inspection logs
  • Traceable calibration reports provided
